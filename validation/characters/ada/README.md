# Ada final evidence

The exact candidate and rebuild instructions are in
`../../../art/characters/candidates/ada/README.md` and `delivery.json`.

- `after/`, `after-head/`: final neutral colour/clay, all directions and original angle.
- `final-review/`: five stroke phases, body/rear/grip/waist/seat/foot views.
- `browser-candidate-*.png`: native-DPR final DQ export in the boat.
- `report.json`, `blender-parity.json`, `body-report.json`, `grip-report.json`: exact-export acceptance.
- `index.html`: headless-compatible interactive candidate/production/June comparison.
- `faceted-surface.ply`, `head-faceted-surface.ply`, `head-colour-layout.npz`: compact rebuild inputs.
- `source-provenance.json`: original and raw inference hashes; raw inference files are not required.

Only files named by the manifest are curated final evidence. Other local folders
are development studies or caches. The neutral benchmark measures synchronous
submission cost; it does not prove Pixel 8 thermal performance.
